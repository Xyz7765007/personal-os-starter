# OS intake

The seven questions that personalise your OS. `/onboard` reads this file, asks whatever is still blank, writes your answers here, and then generates the rest of your setup from it.

You can also fill this in by hand and re-run `/onboard`. Editing an answer and re-running is the normal way to keep your OS current.

**One rule:** the writing samples in Q2 must be pasted from real things you wrote. Not typed fresh, not summarised.

---

Your name, your role, what you make or sell or run, and who it is for. One short paragraph each is plenty.
