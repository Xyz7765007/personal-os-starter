# Context

Evergreen facts about you. `/onboard` creates three files here from your intake:

- `about-me.md` — who you are, your role, the task that eats your week.
- `about-work.md` — what you make or run, who it is for, how the money or results are tracked.
- `priorities.md` — your two or three priorities for the next 90 days.

Edit these by hand whenever they change, or edit `os-intake.md` and re-run `/onboard`. Keep them short. This is what Claude reads first, every session.
