# Decisions log

Append-only. Every meaningful decision goes here with the *why*. `/level-up` writes its scoped automation specs here. You can also add entries by hand, or just tell Claude "log this decision".

Future-you will thank present-you for capturing the reasoning, not just the outcome.

**Format:**

```
## YYYY-MM-DD — Short title

**Decision:** what was decided.

**Why:** the reasoning, the constraints, and what would change your mind.

**Alternatives considered:** what else was on the table.
```
